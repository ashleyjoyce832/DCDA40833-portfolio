Reflection

In this lab I learned from past experience and recycled coding formats I've used before to create a new page. AI helped me implement this, especially when I came across errors, such as having two navigation bars. I understand how to create text, style, and create images for websites. With my knowledge of how to create effective navigation and layout, I asked AI to help code this. For my animation, I created a series of about 7 prompts to get my animation the way I wanted it to look. AI made some errors in creating the symbols, so I had to give it very clear directions and inputs to create this animation. I also experimented with different versions of AI and found the newest models to work best, and realized different models have different strengths and weaknesses. This really showed me how AI can be used as a partner in my work, but not to replace my work, especially when it is confused. I want to continue to learn the fundamentals and utilize AI to help me learn and achieve what I want through this technology.
