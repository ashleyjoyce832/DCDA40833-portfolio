Data Visualization in Tableau Public

Reflection and Analysis

I prepared my data using a Kaggle dataset on global food insecurity. I’m passionate about justice and equality for all, so this topic is very important to me to know which countries are lacking basic needs. I hope to one day be part of initiatives that end world hunger. I used Tablau to create a visualization of the world map, with pinpoints on countries where people are in phase 3 or above, indicating a food security crisis. My goal is for the visualization to show which countries have the highest levels of food insecurity. Based on what I know about this topic, I expected greater prevalence in Africa and other low-income regions. I’m interested to see whether this changes over time or seasonally, for better or worse. I’m also interested in the factors that affect this, including extreme weather events, climate change, and government activity. I’m still a beginner with Tableau and encountered an error when trying to save my map. I plan to learn more about it and can hopefully resolve this error promptly.
